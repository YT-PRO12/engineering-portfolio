# Recruiter-facing portfolio review

This checklist protects the distinction between an accessible demo, its underlying repository, and a verified production product.

## Link and content acceptance

- Check the public homepage and all featured case-study routes with a private browser session.
- Open the official resume PDF and confirm that it is the exact approved asset, legible, and consistently linked.
- Test every publicly exposed certificate link without owner credentials.
- Verify that a *guest walkthrough* says it is synthetic, and never suggests authenticated functionality is open.
- Distinguish external demos from conceptual illustrations and illustrative screenshots.
- Check that resume project names, technologies, and public URL destinations agree with the source of truth.

## Accessibility and reliability

Check narrow and wide viewports, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, page titles, and broken-image fallbacks. Run the documented local build and asset validation before deploying. Recheck public links after publication.

## Evidence policy

Do not invent model accuracy, users, uptime, capacity, certifications, job responsibilities, or test passes. A successful build is not evidence of availability on a hosted domain. Keep this review separate from the actual QA log in [QA_RESULTS.md](QA_RESULTS.md).

This is a **checklist**, not a claim that the listed acceptance steps were executed today.
